What Is Love?

Love is and has always been an important topic for mankind. Through love, we show affection, hope, and gratitude. Some even claim that love is all you need in this world to survive. We find love in many different forms. Maybe you have experienced romantic love, or the love for a mother, father, or child? Love can be related to art, music, pets, food, countries ... the list goes on forever. Moreover, love is the main theme in millions of songs, poems, movies, and novels. Love is all around us!

How can we express love?

Is there such a thing as forbidden love?

What do you simply love doing?

Love can also change lives, and it has the power to change history. Unfortunately, the opposite of love, hate, also has that power.

Can you think of someone who has changed history with love?

Has someone changed history with hate?

All you need is love!

Many artists express their feelings about love, and many of them even claim that all you need is love. Can this be true in a world in which there seem to be more and more conflicts and in which people hurt each other?

Write a text where you:

  • reflect on what love means to you
  • discuss whether love can change the world. Give examples.
  • give your own opinion on the quote "All you need is love!" Do you think that love is all you need? Why / why not?

Give your text a suitable title.
